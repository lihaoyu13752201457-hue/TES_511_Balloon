   839	\section*{Data and software availability}
   840	Before publication, a versioned public repository or institutional archive will
   841	provide the geometry and source definitions, random seeds, analysis and
   842	figure-generation scripts, LaTeX source, and derived validation products needed
   843	to reproduce the tables and figures in this paper. The archive will include
   844	machine-readable summaries and human-readable validation reports for the rate
   845	calculations, convergence checks, figure source tracing, and bibliography
   846	verification. Full raw transport outputs, compressed Cosima event files, and
   847	large intermediate event lists are substantially larger than the manuscript
   848	archive; they will either be deposited as a separate large-data package or
   849	represented in the archive by generation commands, configuration files,
   850	checksums or summary metadata, and derived validation products.
