Because the celestial signal sits at the same energy as atmospheric and instrumental annihilation features, sensitivity cannot be inferred from the lens effective area and detector energy resolution alone; it also depends on the surrounding mass distribution, the active shielding and event selection, the radiation environment, and the accuracy with which the residual background is modeled. Recent bottom-up simulations of the 2016 COSI balloon flight illustrate the level of payload and detector-response modeling---including activation lines and anticoincidence response---needed to validate such a background estimate against flight and calibration data \cite{Gallego2025Balloon,Beechert2022,Ciabattoni2025ACS}.
