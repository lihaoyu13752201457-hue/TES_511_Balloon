# Channel Wall-by-Wall Rebuild

This record documents the first channel model in this repository that does not
force `n_bounce` or a calibrated grazing angle.

## Source Inputs

- 511-CAM public channel inputs: four rings at 2.25, 3.0, 3.75, and 4.5 cm;
  lengths 2.1, 2.7, 3.5, and 4.6 cm; 1 cm wide and 7.5 mm thick segments;
  W/Si bilayers of 30 nm W and 150 nm Si; 12 m focal length.
- Shirazi 2020 channeling lineage: ray tracing used IMD reflectivities and
  spacer absorption terms, with the number of reflections varying from 17 to
  38 for a parallel beam depending on ring length and radius.
- The open fraction is the spacer/period ratio, `150 / (150 + 30) = 0.8333`.

## Implementation

New files:

- `external_baseline/channel_raytrace_py/wallbywall_channel.py`
- `analysis/run_channel_wallbywall_rebuild.py`
- `tests/integration/test_channel_wallbywall_rebuild.py`

The model samples the entrance face of the four ring segments. A photon landing
in the 30 nm W part of a bilayer is rejected by geometry. A photon landing in a
150 nm Si spacer is traced between the two neighboring W/Si interfaces. The
channel bend rotates the local wall tangent, so wall intersections and grazing
angles are generated by geometry. Every wall hit looks up the dense W/Si table.

The same standard optics contract is written:

- `phase_space.csv`
- `optics_history.csv`
- `per_ring_summary.csv/json`
- `summary.json`

## Current Runs

Main run:

```bash
python3 analysis/run_channel_wallbywall_rebuild.py \
  --n 20000 \
  --seed 20260521 \
  --reflectivity-table data/reflectivity/WSi_511keV_parratt_grid_dense.csv \
  --out runs/channel_wallbywall_rebuild
```

Result:

- primaries: 20000
- survived: 5804
- transmissivity: 0.2902
- effective area: 18.4617 cm2
- wall-bounce rows: 189969
- survivor mean bounces: 19.6239
- mean grazing angle: 7.54866e-5 rad
- spot D90: 1.03293 cm

No-Si-path-absorption comparison:

```bash
python3 analysis/run_channel_wallbywall_rebuild.py \
  --n 5000 \

  --no-si-path-absorption \
  --out runs/channel_wallbywall_rebuild_no_si_abs_smoke
```

Result:

- transmissivity: 0.5938
- effective area: 37.7759 cm2
- survivor mean bounces: 21.4015
- mean grazing angle: 7.18637e-5 rad

## Interpretation

This is a real wall-by-wall reconstruction in the sense that reflection count
and grazing angle are not input knobs. It is still not an exact reproduction of
the original 511-CAM IDL result because the original IDL source, assembly
tolerances, and any extra path/open-area accounting are not public.

The most important outcome is that the natural survivor bounce count is now in
the right qualitative range, about 20-21, instead of the old calibrated 1-3.
The price is that the public-geometry model does not reproduce the published
80% optics efficiency. This means the previous suspicion was correct: the
remaining gap is physical/model provenance, not just code plumbing.
