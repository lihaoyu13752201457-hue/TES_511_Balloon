## English caption (EA)

Rate normalization on a common coincidence time axis. (a) Full-band detector event rates (928, 97.1, and 0.987 s^-1). (b) Independent Poisson draws of the three streams are merged and time-ordered; color marks stream identity and hatched bands mark each event's temporal footprint. (c) Events with Δt ≤ τ = 1 μs form one candidate group; the box gives the reference-exposure accidental bookkeeping. Tick positions are schematic.
