Figure X. Mission-integrated narrow-line performance over the 20 d reference
trajectory. Panel a gives the Gaussian counting significance for a continuously
observed top-of-atmosphere flux of 1e-4 ph cm^-2 s^-1. Panel b gives the
Gaussian and Asimov 3σ line-flux thresholds; endpoint error bars show the
1σ Monte Carlo statistical uncertainty of the Gaussian result. The gray line
is the IBIS Gaussian-rescaled 3σ scale, obtained by linearly rescaling the
published 2σ limit; it is not a 3σ value reported by the IBIS
publication. The optimized chimney geometry reaches Gaussian significance
13.46 and a line-flux threshold of (2.229 +/- 0.209)e-5 ph cm^-2 s^-1 at 20 d,
compared with 5.55 and (5.406 +/- 0.401)e-5 ph cm^-2 s^-1 for the reference
geometry. The corresponding Asimov thresholds are 2.238e-5 and 5.415e-5 ph
cm^-2 s^-1.
