Figure X. Matched background cut-flow and performance comparison. Panel a
shows prompt plus delayed background rates in the 510.58--511.42 keV window
before anticoincidence, after the active BGO veto, and after the final topology
selection. The optimized chimney geometry reduces the final direct rate from
0.05430 to 0.009419 s^-1. Panel b gives optimized-to-reference ratios under the
common source, response, selection, and mission definition. The optimized
geometry retains 99.75% of the final effective area, reduces the day-15 mature
background to 18.10%, and reduces the 20 d Gaussian 3-sigma flux threshold to
41.24% of the reference value.
