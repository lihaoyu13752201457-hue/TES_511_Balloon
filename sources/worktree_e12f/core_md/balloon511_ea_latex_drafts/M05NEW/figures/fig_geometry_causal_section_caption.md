Figure X. Lateral displacement of the TES from the cryogenic stages. The
reference geometry places the TES active layers within the vertical projection
of the mixing chamber and staged cold plates. The optimized geometry places
the TES in a side chimney surrounded by active BGO while retaining the focused
photon path. Symbols show the exact production positions of delayed events that
pass the final selection in the 510.58--511.42 keV window; symbol area follows
the day-15 rate weight. The fraction originating in the mixing chamber and cold
plates decreases from 32.18% to 3.92%. Small supports and constructive-solid-
geometry apertures not needed for this comparison are omitted.
